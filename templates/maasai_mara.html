DISCOVER THE MARA

An unforgettable Safari Experience

Maasai Mara is a wildlife paradise in southwestern Kenya. It is famed for the Great Migration, when millions of wildebeest, zebras and gazelles move across the region.

The reserve offers thrilling safari experiences, spectacular landscapes and opportunities to see the Big Five and many other species.

Beyond the wildlife, visitors can experience the beauty of the vast savannahs, Maasai culture and unforgettable African sunsets.
